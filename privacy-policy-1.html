Privacy Policy

MEEM Hub — Mechanical Engineering • Energetics & Maintenance

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

MEEM Hub is a Telegram bot designed to provide educational resources and tools for Mechanical Engineering students, including course materials, schedules, professor information, file requests, reports, favorites, search, and related academic features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot stores, why it is used, and how it is handled.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the bot, MEEM Hub may store:

  • Telegram user information: your Telegram user ID, display name, username (when available), and the time you last interacted with the bot.
  • Favorites: files that you choose to save as favorites.
  • Usage statistics: searches performed, subject views, and file download counts.
  • Messages you submit: complaints, suggestions, technical reports, and requests for missing files.
  • Report information: when you report a file, the bot may store the reported file reference, file name, report category, your Telegram name/username, and any additional details you provide.

If you submit a complaint using the anonymous option, your name and username are not displayed to the administration in that complaint. However, the bot still requires your Telegram account identifier to process the interaction and allow the administration to reply.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored by MEEM Hub is used to:

  • Provide the bot's educational and library features.
  • Remember your favorites.
  • Process complaints, suggestions, file requests, and file reports.
  • Maintain basic usage statistics and improve the service.
  • Send administrative announcements to users who have interacted with the bot.
  • Maintain backups of the bot's operational data.

4. Telegram

MEEM Hub operates through Telegram. When you interact with the bot, Telegram may provide information to the bot according to Telegram's own services and policies. MEEM Hub does not control information that Telegram independently processes.

For information about Telegram's own privacy practices, please consult Telegram's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage

The bot stores operational data locally in its database. The bot also creates periodic backups of this operational data for recovery purposes. Access to administrative data is restricted to the bot administrator.

No payment information, passwords, or financial account information is intentionally collected by MEEM Hub.

6. File and Educational Content

Files uploaded to the bot's educational library may contain information supplied by administrators or other authorized contributors. Users should avoid submitting confidential, private, or sensitive personal information inside files or messages.

7. Data Sharing

MEEM Hub does not intentionally sell personal information. Information submitted through complaints, reports, or file requests may be visible to the bot administrator when necessary to operate and manage the service.

Information may also be processed by Telegram as part of delivering Telegram's platform and bot services.

8. Data Retention

Data may be retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary to operate the bot, maintain its educational library, handle user communications, maintain statistics, and keep backups. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and operational requirements.

9. Your Choices

You can stop using the bot at any time. You should not send information through the bot that you do not want stored for the purposes described in this policy.

If you have a question about information associated with your use of MEEM Hub or want to request deletion or correction of information, contact the MEEM Hub administrator through the bot.

10. Children's Privacy

MEEM Hub is intended for university-level educational use. The service is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

11. Security

Reasonable measures are used to restrict administrative access to the bot's stored data. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at the same public policy URL.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the MEEM Hub administrator through the MEEM Hub Telegram bot.
